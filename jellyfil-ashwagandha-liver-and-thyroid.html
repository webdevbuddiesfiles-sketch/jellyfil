Ashwagandha, Green Tea, The Liver And The Thyroid

Two of the eight ingredients need a liver and thyroid check.*

Ashwagandha

Rare cases of liver injury have been reported, and ashwagandha can raise thyroid hormone levels. It may also add to sedatives, diabetes medicine and immune-suppressing drugs.

Green Tea Extract

Concentrated extracts have been linked to liver injury, which matters if you drink alcohol regularly or take other liver-affecting products.

Ask First If You

  • Have liver disease or take liver-affecting medicine
  • Take thyroid medicine or have a thyroid condition
  • Take sedatives, sleep aids or immune-suppressing drugs

Stop And Get Help If

You notice yellow skin or eyes, dark urine, pale stools, strong tiredness, nausea or pain in the upper right belly.
