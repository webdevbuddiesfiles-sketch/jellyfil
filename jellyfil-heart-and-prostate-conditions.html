Heart, Prostate And Hormone-Sensitive Conditions

Men's vitality products are not neutral for every man.*

Heart Conditions

Horny goat weed may affect heart rhythm and blood pressure, caffeine can speed the heart, and L-arginine can lower pressure. Ask first with heart disease, low blood pressure, an arrhythmia or after a heart attack.

Prostate And Hormone-Sensitive Conditions

Tribulus, ashwagandha and horny goat weed are marketed for hormone effects. Ask first with prostate cancer, a history of hormone-sensitive cancer or testosterone treatment.

Diabetes And Surgery

Ashwagandha and tribulus may lower blood sugar, and several ingredients may affect bleeding. Tell your surgeon about every supplement.
