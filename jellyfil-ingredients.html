How to read it: eight named ingredients share a single 82 mg blend, which is about 10 mg each if split evenly, and the split is not published. Compare the Supplement Facts panel on your own bottle for the serving size, other ingredients and allergens.

Directions (seller text): one gummy a day, ideally at the same time each day with a glass of water. Consult your physician before use if you take medication, have a medical condition or are sensitive to caffeine.

Seller amount: in the 82 mg blend

1. Maca Extract

A Peruvian root vegetable long eaten for stamina and fertility.

Studied for: Small trials on sexual desire and mood.

Worth knowing: Effects look modest, and amounts used in trials are grams, not milligrams.

Seller amount: in the 82 mg blend

2. Muira Puama Extract

A Brazilian shrub bark nicknamed potency wood in folk medicine.

Studied for: A few small, older studies on desire.

Worth knowing: Evidence is thin.

Seller amount: in the 82 mg blend

3. Catuaba Extract

Bark of Brazilian trees used traditionally as a tonic.

Studied for: Mostly traditional use and lab work.

Worth knowing: Almost no human trials.

Seller amount: in the 82 mg blend

4. Horny Goat Weed

Epimedium leaf, whose icariin has been studied in the lab.

Studied for: Human evidence is limited.

Worth knowing: May affect blood pressure, heart rhythm and blood thinning.

Seller amount: in the 82 mg blend

5. Tribulus Terrestris

A spiny plant used in men's formulas for decades.

Studied for: Trials find little change in testosterone.

Worth knowing: May lower blood sugar and affect hormone-sensitive conditions.

Seller amount: in the 82 mg blend

6. Ashwagandha

An Indian adaptogen root used for stress and vigor.

Studied for: Small trials on stress, sleep and testosterone in some men.

Worth knowing: Rare liver injury and thyroid effects have been reported.

Seller amount: in the 82 mg blend

7. L-Arginine

An amino acid the body uses to make nitric oxide.

Studied for: Trials use several grams a day for blood flow.

Worth knowing: Can lower blood pressure and matters with nitrates and ED medicines.

Seller amount: in the 82 mg blend

8. Green Tea Extract

A concentrated extract of green tea catechins.

Studied for: Small effects on energy and weight.

Worth knowing: Concentrated extracts have been linked to liver injury.

Seller amount: 5 mg

9. Natural Caffeine

A mild stimulant, about a twentieth of a cup of coffee.

Studied for: Alertness at larger amounts.

Worth knowing: Small, but add it to any other caffeine you take.
